I am a graphic designer based in Limassol, Cyprus. For six years I have been building visual identities, campaign assets, and digital experiences for companies that refuse to look like their competitors.

Currently Junior Graphic Designer within the Marketing Department of Fameline Holding Group — designing across an entire corporate group, from full rebranding systems to motion, social media, and web. Before that, six years as Digital Marketing & Systems Coordinator at P.K. Trisel Electronics, owning everything from newsletters to the company website.

I work across the full spectrum of visual communication: logo and brand identity, editorial and print, motion graphics and video production, social media content, and web design. The medium changes. The standard does not.

Before a design leaves my desk, it has been tested against one question: does this make the right thing easier? I am not interested in decoration. I am interested in clarity, confidence, and the kind of work people keep for decades.

VERIFIED

Trained at Frederick University Limassol in Graphic and Advertising Design — with an Erasmus+ year at Escuela de Arte Sevilla, Spain. An immersion in Spanish visual culture that permanently altered my sense of space, colour, and rhythm.

I bring the same energy to a brand relaunch as to a single social post. Every touchpoint is a chance to be precise. Every brief is a puzzle that has exactly one honest answer — and several comfortable ones I will not deliver.

Tools: Illustrator · InDesign · Photoshop · After Effects · Premiere Pro · WordPress. Languages: Greek, English, Spanish.
