Overview

A single-page launch site for MiaFemtech Cyprus — the minimally-invasive breast aesthetics brand — announcing its next-generation biocompatible implants. The page had to feel premium, reassuring and unmistakably feminine, while walking a prospective patient from headline to consultation booking in one scroll.

I designed the full landing page end to end: hero, product story, the 8-step procedure, benefits, an interactive Before & After, surgeon profiles and contact — built on the Mia brand system of soft blush gradients, fine floral linework and an elegant serif voice, with a single rose accent driving every call to action.

The design was delivered as a complete responsive set — desktop, tablet and mobile — with a full guidelines spec (typography, colour and assets) for hand-off to development.
