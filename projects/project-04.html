Overview

An end-to-end design programme for MiaFemtech Cyprus — the minimally-invasive breast aesthetics brand — spanning a premium launch landing page and an ongoing social-media campaign, all built on the Mia brand system of soft blush gradients, fine floral linework and an elegant serif voice.

The landing page walks a prospective patient from headline to consultation in one responsive scroll. The social media runs as a monthly content calendar across recurring pillars — The Experience Series, Myth vs Reality, Behind the Brand — alongside campaign moments like the Motiva Flora Awards and launch videos.

Below: the website & responsive layouts, the guidelines spec, the Motiva Flora Awards activation, the video reels, and the running content calendar.
