Overview

“Χέρι με Χέρι” — Hand in Hand Children’s Charity Foundation — supports children and families in need across Cyprus, under the promise “one smile at a time.”

Fameline Holding Group proudly backs the foundation through its giving campaign, Bundle of Joy — boxes and bags of food and everyday essentials packed for families in need.

Process Notes

01
The Cause

Hand in Hand supports children and families in need — the work had to feel warm and hopeful, never clinical.

02
The Look

Soft blue and warm coral, real product photography and a hand-lettered “Bundle of Joy” mark for a crafted, human feel.

03

Rolled out as a set of campaign posts and a LinkedIn carousel of the bags — hand-decorated by children.
