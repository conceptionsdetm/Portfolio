Overview

“Χέρι με Χέρι” — Hand in Hand Children’s Charity Foundation — supports children and families in need across Cyprus, under the promise “one smile at a time.”

Fameline Holding Group proudly backs the foundation, and the design work runs across its identity, its roll-up banners and collateral, and the group’s own giving campaign, Bundle of Joy — food and essentials packed for families in need.
