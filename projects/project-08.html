Overview

Navichem is a marine-chemicals brand supplying cleaning, water-treatment and maintenance products to ships worldwide. The identity had to feel scientific and trustworthy while reading clearly on a moving deck.

The mark is a wave-and-current “N” set in a sphere — the sea in motion. From there the system runs across GHS-compliant product labels, a marine product catalogue and full stationery, in deep navy and marine cyan.
