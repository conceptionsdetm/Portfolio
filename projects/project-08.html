Overview

Navichem is a marine-chemicals brand supplying cleaning, water-treatment and maintenance products to ships worldwide. The identity had to feel scientific and trustworthy while reading clearly on a moving deck.

The mark is a wave-and-current “N” set in a sphere — the sea in motion. From there the system runs across GHS-compliant product labels, a marine product catalogue, stationery and a launch website (navi-chem.com), in deep navy and marine cyan.

Process Notes

01
The Mark

Two currents wrap into an “N” inside a sphere — water in motion, a globe of marine reach, refined over six proposal rounds.

02
System

Deep navy and marine cyan with a clean geometric sans, built to stay legible from a product drum to a catalogue spread.

03
Applied

Taken onto GHS-compliant labels — pictograms, hazard statements and net volume — and a 10-page marine product catalogue.
