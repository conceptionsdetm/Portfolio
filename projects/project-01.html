Overview

Fameline Holding Group undertook a full visual identity relaunch — a rebranding exercise that unified the group's communication across every touchpoint, from printed stationery to digital presence, internal signage, and event assets.

The challenge was to create a cohesive, authoritative identity system that could flex across the group's diverse subsidiaries while maintaining a clear and premium holding-company voice. Every deliverable was designed in parallel, ensuring consistency across print, digital, and environmental applications.

Below is the complete Launch Plan — each deliverable presented individually in order of production.

01 — Letterhead

The official A4 letterhead for Fameline Holding Group. Designed with the new brand identity, featuring the updated logo, corporate typography, and the distinctive brand stripe. Produced in both contact and no-contact variants for internal and external use.

02 — Business Cards

Business card system for the Holding Group, featuring the updated brand mark, corporate colour palette, and standardised layout across all group personnel. Printed in high-quality stock with a clean, professional finish.

03 & 04 — A4 & DL Envelopes

03
A4 Envelope

Large-format envelope for documents and printed materials, branded with the FHG identity and return address layout.

04
DL Envelope

Standard DL envelope for correspondence — matching brand language and print-ready specifications.

05 — Compliments Slip

A compact compliments slip designed for inclusion with physical correspondence. Carries the full brand identity in a concise format — logo, stripe, and minimal contact information.

06 — A4 Notepad

Branded A4 notepad for the group — featuring the FHG logo, brand stripe, and a clean grid layout suitable for meeting notes and internal use. Produced in pad format for desk placement.

07 — LinkedIn Banner

LinkedIn cover banner for the official Fameline Holding Group company page. Designed to platform dimensions of 1584 × 396 px, communicating the group's new visual identity to a professional digital audience.

08 — Email Signatures

Standardised HTML email signature system deployed across all Fameline Holding Group personnel. Features the new brand logo and stripe, ensuring every outgoing email carries a consistent professional identity. Available in variants for each subsidiary.

09 — Desktop Backgrounds

Branded desktop wallpaper for use across all group workstations. Multiple variants produced — including vessel imagery with central logo, and an abstracted brand pattern — ensuring internal screens reflect the new identity.

10 — Teams Backdrop

A branded virtual background for Microsoft Teams video calls. Multiple backdrops were produced using generative imagery blended with the FHG visual identity — maintaining a professional, on-brand appearance for all virtual meetings.

11 — Rollup Banner

Freestanding rollup banner for events, exhibitions, and reception areas. Designed to standard 85 × 200 cm dimensions, showcasing the refreshed FHG brand at full scale. Four presentation variants were explored before final approval.

12 — Strategy Presentation Template

A fully rebranded PowerPoint template for internal and external presentations. Slide backgrounds, typography, and layout guides were redesigned to reflect the new brand system — ensuring all future presentations carry a consistent FHG identity.
