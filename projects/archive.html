The Record

Across nearly three years in-house at the Fameline Holding Group, I was the designer behind the full creative output of 40+ sister brands — from maritime and lubricants to PPE, logistics, chemicals and charity. This is the record: the websites, brand systems, brochures, decks, campaigns, events and merch that went out the door with my hands on them.
