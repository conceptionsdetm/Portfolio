Overview

An ongoing social-media campaign for Amsoil Cyprus — the synthetic-lubricants brand distributed on the island by Ollets Ltd. I run the monthly content calendar end to end: concept, copy direction, design and motion, across Instagram and Facebook.

The work is organised as a rolling monthly calendar. Each month mixes product spotlights, educational posts, seasonal angles, ambassador features and live-event coverage — all held together by one bold, high-contrast Amsoil visual language built for the feed.

Below is the strategy board the campaign is built on, the monthly creations grouped by month, and the Classic Car Festival activation. Several posts also ship as motion / video pieces.
