Overview

An ongoing social-media campaign for Amsoil Cyprus — the synthetic-lubricants brand distributed on the island by Ollets Ltd. I run the monthly content calendar end to end: concept, copy direction, design and motion, across Instagram and Facebook.

The work is organised as a rolling monthly calendar. Each month mixes product spotlights, educational carousels, seasonal angles, ambassador features, live-event coverage and motion pieces — all in one bold, high-contrast Amsoil visual language built for the feed.

Process Notes

01
Calendar

Plan each month from the strategy board — balancing product spotlights, education, seasonal hooks, ambassadors and events.

02
System

One Amsoil visual language — bold type, high-contrast product photography and the red/blue brand mark — applied consistently across every post.

Static + Motion

Deliver each slot for Instagram and Facebook as multi-slide carousels, stories and, where it lands harder, animated / video pieces.
