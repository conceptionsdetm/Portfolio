Overview

An ongoing social-media campaign for Amsoil Cyprus — the synthetic-lubricants brand distributed on the island by Ollets Ltd. I run the monthly content calendar end to end: concept, copy direction, design and motion, across Instagram and Facebook.

The work is organised as a rolling monthly calendar. Each month mixes product spotlights, educational carousels, seasonal angles, ambassador features, live-event coverage and motion pieces — all held together by one bold, high-contrast Amsoil visual language built for the feed.

Most posts are multi-slide carousels — click through each one below. Motion posts play inline as video.
