Overview

Diving deep for vessel performance.

North Tide is a marine services specialist and a member of the MIE Group, delivering underwater inspections, hull cleaning, propeller polishing, afloat repairs and Ultrasonic Thickness Measurement (UTM) to vessels worldwide — round-the-clock, through a vetted global network of class-approved contractors.

The work covered two connected deliverables: a single-page marketing website and a corporate profile presentation, both built on the same navy-and-teal system and the diagonal “tide-line” graphic drawn from the logo.

Process Notes

01
The Mark

The logo splits “North” in navy from “Tide” in teal, with a chevron cut through the N — a waterline that becomes the brand’s recurring diagonal across web and print.

02
The Website

One continuous scroll tells the whole story — a diver hero, the company, a world map of port coverage, services and contact — kept fast and legible, and reworked for tablet and mobile.

03
The Deck

The profile presentation reuses the exact colour and diagonal system so the sales conversation and the website feel like one brand from first contact to closing slide.
