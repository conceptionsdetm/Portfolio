Overview

A complete e-commerce design for Fluxotic — an automotive and industrial lubricants retailer carrying AMSOIL and other premium oil brands across Cyprus. The brief was a full online store that feels fast, trustworthy and built for people who know their machines.

I designed the entire customer journey end to end: a bold homepage, a filterable product catalogue, single-product pages, the full cart and checkout flow, customer accounts, and the surrounding Blog, Contact and About pages — all tied together by a layered mega-menu and a matching set of transactional emails.

The system runs on a dark, industrial palette with a single confident accent, big product photography, and clear filtering — so a workshop owner can find the exact fluid for the exact vehicle in a couple of clicks.

Process Notes

01
Structure

Mapped the full catalogue — vehicles, activities, oils and chemicals — into a filter and mega-menu system so any product is reachable in a couple of clicks.

02
Visual Language

Built a dark, industrial UI with one strong accent, heavy display type and large product photography to match the performance positioning of the brands sold.

03
Full Journey

Designed every state of the journey — browse, filter, product, cart, checkout, account and the follow-up emails — as one consistent system.
