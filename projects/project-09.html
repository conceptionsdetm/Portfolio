Overview

The Famelions are Fameline Holding Group’s beach-volley team, formed to play the CSC Cyprus Shipping Chamber Charity Tournament 2026 in aid of the “Hand in Hand” children’s charity.

The brief was a whole team identity and its rollout: a crowned-lion crest, a run of social announcements from recruitment to game day, custom volleyball kits, and on-court event branding — feather flags and cooler-box graphics.
