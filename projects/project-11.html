Overview

Movember is the global movement for men’s health — prostate and testicular cancer, mental health and suicide prevention. For 2025, Fameline Holding Group ran its own campaign under the line “Men’s health matters, get involved.”

The work spans awareness posts, an animated invitation to an in-house fundraiser — a 2v2 table-football tournament with a pop-up barbershop, DJ and cocktails — and a thank-you reel once the moustaches came off.
