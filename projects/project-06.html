Overview

A complete brand identity and launch site for Albaflux — a cargo-logistics company whose promise is “Navigating the Pulse of Trade.” The work covers the full brand system and its first digital touchpoint.

The brand guidelines set the logo, the arrow-and-route mark, the deep-navy palette, typography and applications; the landing page puts that system to work in a clean, confident one-scroll site.
