Overview

A corporate presentation for BoltCargo Logistics — a logistics partner supporting ship managers, vessel by vessel. The deck introduces the company, its services and its network to prospective clients.

The design pairs a confident blue-to-teal system with clean typographic hierarchy, aerial vessel photography and a signature “B” bolt mark carried across ten 16:9 slides.

Figures, records and body copy are intentionally blurred here for client confidentiality — the layout and visual system are shown as designed.

Process Notes

01
The System

A blue-to-teal gradient, a bold sans and a repeating “B” bolt watermark give the deck a consistent, energetic spine from cover to close.

02
The Structure

Ten 16:9 slides move from company intro to services, network and delivery — each built on a clear grid so dense information stays readable.

03
The Detail

Aerial vessel photography and accent rules tie the logistics story to the sea, keeping a technical subject visually engaging.
