Overview

Delivering peace of mind to ship managers, one vessel at a time.

Blue Sky Cargo Logistics (BSCL) is a Limassol-based marine logistics partner that acts as an embedded, independent control tower for ship managers — handling freight forwarding, spare-parts coordination, warehousing and last-mile delivery to vessels in port or at anchorage.

The engagement covered the full identity: a logo built around a forward-moving chevron, a navy-and-sky colour system, stationery, and a ten-slide corporate profile that carries the brand from cover to contact.

Process Notes

01
The Direction

Four routes were explored — a literal container monogram and a typographic wordmark. The forward chevron won: it reads as motion, an arrow and a cargo pennant all at once, and stays sharp at any size.

02
The System

A deep sky-navy paired with two brighter marine blues gives the brand both authority and openness — “blue sky” made literal. The chevron facet becomes a recurring graphic device across every layout.

03
The Profile

The company deck translates the identity into a sales tool — diagonal chevron windows frame vessel and port photography, while a strict grid keeps a service-heavy story clear and confident.
