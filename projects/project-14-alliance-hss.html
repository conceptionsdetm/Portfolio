Overview

Alliance HSS — a joint venture between Alliance Safety and HSS Marine Safety — supplies maritime safety and firefighting equipment worldwide. I handled the brand’s key 2026 touchpoints: the Asia Pacific Maritime (APM) 2026 Singapore exhibition presence and the website landing page.

One bold, high-contrast system — navy, orange and signal yellow over real safety photography and certification marks — carried from a 3.6×2.4m booth wall onto the full single-page site.
