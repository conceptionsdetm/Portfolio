Overview

VessGuard is a marine-grade PPE brand — the crew’s guardian, protecting seafarers from deck to port with quality-assured safety gear.

The work pairs a logo system built on a shield-and-“VG” mark with a product brochure that carries the brand across boiler suits, safety footwear, gloves, parkas and certified coveralls — industrial confidence in charcoal and signal red.
